About the Fund

What is an SPV?

An SPV (Special Purpose Vehicle) is a legal entity created for a single investment purpose. When you invest through Liftoff Partners Fund I, your capital is pooled with other investors to collectively hold an indirect interest in SpaceX.

You do not hold SpaceX shares directly. You hold an interest in the SPV, which holds those interests on your behalf. This is the same structure used by institutional investors and family offices to access private markets.

Harwick Securities acts as placement agent. All investor funds are held in a segregated escrow account at North Capital Trust Company and are deployed only upon a successful fund closing. You will receive full confirmation and documentation of your SPV interest.

FAQ

Common Questions,
Straight Answers

What is an SPV?
An SPV (Special Purpose Vehicle) is a legal entity created for a single investment purpose. When you invest through Liftoff Partners Fund I, your money is pooled with other investors to collectively hold an indirect interest in SpaceX. This is the same structure used by institutional investors to access private markets. You hold an interest in the SPV, which holds SpaceX interests on your behalf.
When is SpaceX expected to start trading?
SpaceX officially filed for its IPO on June 4, 2026, and will trade on the NASDAQ under the ticker SPCX. Public trading is expected to begin shortly after the SEC review process is complete. Our SPV aims to secure shares at the set pre-trading price of $135/share.
What happens if the IPO is delayed or withdrawn?
While SpaceX has officially filed, market conditions or regulatory reviews can sometimes delay trading. If the IPO is withdrawn, your shares will remain held in the SPV until another liquidity event (such as a secondary market transaction or acquisition) occurs.
How do I exit my investment?
Exits are typically triggered by a SpaceX IPO, acquisition, or a secondary market transaction facilitated by Harwick Securities. All SPV investors will be notified of any liquidity events. Early exits outside of these events are not guaranteed and may not be available. SPV interests are generally illiquid.
Is this investment risky?
Yes. All private market investments carry significant risk. Investing in SpaceX through this SPV involves illiquidity, market uncertainty, and the real possibility of losing your entire investment. We encourage all investors to carefully review the full offering documents and consult a qualified financial, legal, and tax advisor before investing.
What is the minimum investment?
You can participate starting at $1,500. This offering is structured under Regulation A+ (Tier II), which allows investments from all U.S. investors regardless of accreditation status, with no individual investment limits imposed by the exemption.
How are investor funds held?
All investor funds are held in a segregated escrow account maintained by North Capital Trust Company, a qualified third-party custodian. Funds are deployed into the SPV only upon a successful closing. You will receive written confirmation and complete documentation of your SPV interest following the close date.
Who holds my money before the SPV closes?
Your funds are held in escrow by North Capital Trust Company, an independent qualified escrow agent. Funds are only released to the SPV upon a successful close on June 9, 2026. If the offering does not close for any reason, your funds will be returned to you in full. No fees charged.
What is North Capital Trust Company?
North Capital Trust Company is a registered transfer agent and escrow provider specializing in private securities offerings. They provide independent custody of investor funds, ensuring your money is protected and segregated throughout the subscription process until the SPV closing date of June 9, 2026.
What happens if the $1,000,000 target raise is not met?
If the minimum raise threshold is not met by the close date of June 9, 2026, all funds held in escrow by North Capital Trust Company will be returned to investors in full. No fees will be charged and you will be notified promptly. Your funds will be released without deduction.
